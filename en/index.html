How certification prep works today

Three bottlenecks almost every startup hits on its first security certification. The problem isn't effort — it's the method.

One person, 416 hours

Gathering evidence from every team and mapping it to criteria by hand. 52 business days a year disappear into paperwork.

Controls trapped in spreadsheets

Hundreds of controls live in one spreadsheet and one person's head. When they leave, the certification wobbles.

Once-a-year crunch

Everything crammed in right before the audit. The rest of the year, no one knows what security actually protects.
